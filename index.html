How to read it

Attributes

Touch
Every on-ball event logged at its pitch location — passes, receptions, carries, shots, tackles, clearances. Counted per hex, normalised per 90 minutes.
Coverage
Share of the player's on-pitch time their tracked position sat inside the hex (from 25 Hz positional data). Only zones with touches above the threshold (relative to the player's busiest hex) are drawn, so the map shows where the player is active rather than filling the pitch.
Core area
Smallest set of hexes holding 80% of the player's time, in m².
Avg. position
Touch-weighted centroid, shown as the numbered marker.
